Overview

This document illustrates the UNTP (United Nations Transparency Protocol) value chain for the Neocycle project, showing two parallel processing paths from rare earth supplier through upcycling to downstream manufacturing:

  • 🔵 SSC Path (July 2024): Crushed NdFeB Granulate → Solid-State Chlorination
  • 🟠 ECEX Path (August 2024): Whole NdFeB Magnets → Electrochemical Extraction

📊 Note on Realistic Values: All quantity ratios are based on current technology (2024-2026) using Nd32 grade NdFeB magnets (32% Nd, 65% Fe, 4% Dy, 1% B). SSC achieves 90% recovery efficiency (established technology), while ECEX achieves 85% recovery efficiency (emerging technology). Mass balances comply with conservation of mass: SSC recovers 92% total mass (6.15/6.7 KGM), ECEX recovers 87% (5.81/6.7 KGM), with losses attributed to slag, dust, and process residues.

📚 Acronyms & Terminology

Supply Chain Terms:

  • WEEE: Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment - End-of-life electronics containing valuable materials
  • EOL: End-of-Life - Products that have reached the end of their useful service period
  • QC: Quality Control - Samples retained for testing and verification (0.05 KGM per batch)
  • KGM: Kilogram - Standard mass unit (1 KGM = 1,000 grams)
  • Batch: Discrete production lot with unique identifier (e.g., NDFEB-GRAN-2024-07-003)

Processing Technologies:

  • SSC: Solid-State Chlorination - Thermal process using NH₄Cl + N₂ to extract rare earths
  • ECEX: Electrochemical Extraction - Ambient temperature electrolytic separation process
  • NdFeB: Neodymium-Iron-Boron - Permanent magnet alloy used in electronics

Chemical Elements:

  • Nd: Neodymium - Rare earth element used in catalysts and magnets (atomic #60)
  • Fe: Iron - Ferrous metal, majority component of NdFeB magnets
  • Dy: Dysprosium - Heavy rare earth element for high-temperature applications
  • NH₃: Ammonia - Alkaline gas used in fertilizers and chemical synthesis
  • H₂: Hydrogen - Clean energy carrier and industrial feedstock
  • B: Boron - Metalloid element, minor component in NdFeB alloys

UNTP Credentials:

  • UNTP: United Nations Transparency Protocol - Global standard for supply chain traceability (v0.7.0)
  • DPP: Digital Product Passport - W3C Verifiable Credential containing product data and provenance
  • DTE: Digital Traceability Event - Record of transformation, transaction, or observation events
  • DCC: Digital Conformity Credential - Certification of standards compliance (ISO, industry specs)
  • DFR: Digital Facility Record - Verified information about production facilities
  • DIA: Digital Identity Anchor - Organizational identity verification (LEI, business registration)

Technical Standards:

  • W3C: World Wide Web Consortium - Standards body for Verifiable Credentials v2
  • UNCEFACT: UN Centre for Trade Facilitation and Electronic Business - Data vocabulary provider
  • GS1: Global Standards 1 - GTIN product identification system
  • GLEIF: Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation - LEI registry operator
  • LEI: Legal Entity Identifier - 20-character unique organization identifier
  • SIRENE: French business registration system
  • DID: Decentralized Identifier - W3C standard for verifiable digital identity

Circular Economy Metrics:

  • MCI: Material Circularity Index - Ellen MacArthur Foundation metric (0-1 scale, 0.67 = high)
  • Recycled Content: Percentage of material from post-consumer/post-industrial waste (80-86%)
  • Utility Factor: Product lifetime extension vs. industry average (1.2 = 20% longer)
  • GHG Intensity: Greenhouse gas emissions per kg of product (kg CO₂-eq/kg)

Downstream Products:

  • FeNd Catalyst: Iron-Neodymium catalyst for ammonia synthesis (Haber-Bosch process)
  • Polymerization Catalyst: Nd-based catalyst for polymer chain growth reactions
  • API: Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient - Drug synthesis intermediate requiring Nd catalysts
  • Suzuki Reaction: Carbon-carbon bond forming reaction using Nd catalysts (pharma/materials)

Traceability Concepts:

  • Batch Tracking: Following material lot from origin through transformation to final product
  • Provenance: Complete history of product origin, custody, and transformation
  • Mass Balance: Verification that inputs = outputs at each process stage
  • upstreamProcess: Custom UNTP extension for recording SSC vs ECEX path attribution
  • Verifiable Credential: Cryptographically signed digital certificate (W3C VC standard)
  • SHA-256: Cryptographic hash function for data integrity verification

Organizational Identifiers:

  • NDFEB-GRAN-2024-07-003: SSC path batch - Crushed granulate, July 2024, batch #3
  • NDFEB-MAG-2024-08-004: ECEX path batch - Whole magnets, August 2024, batch #4
  • 549300RERC123456789: Example LEI format for Rare Earth Recycling Ltd
  • FR123456789: Example SIRENE number for French organizations
